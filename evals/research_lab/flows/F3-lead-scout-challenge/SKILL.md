# F3 Lead–Scout–Challenge

1. Lead defines independent claims and evidence requirements.
2. Dispatch scouts with non-overlapping questions and exact source scopes.
3. Lead builds a claim-to-evidence synthesis from scout returns.
4. Dispatch one challenger to identify unsupported claims, contradictions, stale evidence, and citation mismatch in that synthesis.
5. Lead corrects the synthesis and returns supported conclusions plus unresolved conflicts.
